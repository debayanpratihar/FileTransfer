# AI Notebook
## Product Vision

Version: 1.0

---

# Vision Statement

AI Notebook is a next-generation offline AI notebook that combines natural handwriting, professional note-taking, and local artificial intelligence into a single seamless experience.

The goal is not to create another chat application or another notes application.

The goal is to create an intelligent notebook where AI becomes another person writing beside the user.

The notebook itself is the conversation.

There should never be a feeling of "switching to AI."

The AI should feel naturally integrated into the notebook.

---

# Privacy Philosophy

Most note-taking apps cannot understand handwriting.

Most AI apps cannot understand handwritten notes.

Not the main character.

# AI Should Feel Human

they should appear naturally.

The AI should write.

Not type.

# AI Should Respect The User

AI never overwrites notes.

AI never hides drawings.

AI never modifies user handwriting.

AI writes beside existing content.

If there is insufficient space

AI automatically creates additional writing space.

---

# Manual AI

Users should always have a Generate button.

Generate means

Read current notebook context.

Understand handwriting.

Understand drawings.

Understand diagrams.

Generate the best possible response.

Write directly inside notebook.

---

# Automatic AI

Users can enable

Automatic AI.

When enabled

AI observes writing.

After the user pauses writing

AI begins generating automatically.

Users can disable this permanently.

Manual mode must always exist.
