
# AI Notebook - Product Roadmap & Future Features

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision, roadmap, strategic milestones, and planned future capabilities for AI Notebook. It serves as a guide for future development while ensuring that new features remain compatible with the application's offline-first philosophy.

---

# Product Vision

AI Notebook aims to become the most capable offline AI-powered notebook for Android, combining handwritten notes, intelligent assistance, drawing tools, and local AI into a single seamless experience.

Core values:

- Offline-first
- Privacy-first
- AI as an assistant, not a replacement
- Professional-grade note-taking
- Long-term maintainability

---

# Guiding Principles

Every new feature should:

- Preserve notebook performance
- Protect user privacy
- Keep handwritten notes central
- Avoid unnecessary complexity
- Remain optional whenever possible

---
